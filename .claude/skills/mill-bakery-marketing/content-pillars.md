# Content Pillars

Every post falls into one of these six categories. Aim for a mix of 2–3 different pillars per week.

---

## 1. Menu Spotlight (~30% of posts)
**Goal**: Surface a specific item people can come buy.

**Format**:
- Item name (big, all caps, Bree Serif heavy)
- Short description (1 line)
- Price (mono, smaller)
- Background: cream OR existing gallery photo with overlay

**Example headlines**:
- "CHORIZO BURRITO"
- "CONCHA + COFFEE"
- "PAN DULCE — DAILY"

**Caption template**: `{item name} — {one-line description}. {hours line}.`

---

## 2. Hours / Location (~15% of posts)
**Goal**: Remind people we exist and when to come.

**Format**:
- Big "5 AM" or "OPEN DAILY"
- Address subtle
- Plain background, red type

**Example headlines**:
- "OPEN 5 AM"
- "DAILY 5–1"
- "MORNINGS ON MACARTHUR"

**Caption template**: `open daily 5 am — 1 pm. 116 w macarthur blvd, santa ana. stop in early ☀️`

---

## 3. Neighborhood / Personality (~15% of posts)
**Goal**: Build emotional connection. Reinforce "local" identity.

**Format**:
- Quote-style serif headline
- Subtle accent (gold underline)
- Background: cream or warm photo

**Example headlines**:
- "Santa Ana's morning spot"
- "The usual? coming up."
- "Where MacArthur wakes up"

**Caption template**: `{tagline}. {open 5 am} · {address city}.`

---

## 4. Process / Craft (~15% of posts)
**Goal**: Convey care + freshness without claiming it overtly.

**Format**:
- Photo-led OR typographic
- Headline: "Made this morning" / "Fresh from the case"

**Example headlines**:
- "FRESH THIS MORNING"
- "STILL WARM"
- "BAKED BEFORE DAWN"

**Caption template**: `pan dulce tray, somewhere around 6 am. {item or two}, {item}. open until 1.`

---

## 5. Hero Photo (~15% of posts)
**Goal**: Repurpose the existing gallery photos with editorial captions.

**Format**:
- Full-bleed photo (one of the 8 in `assets/gallery/`)
- Bottom gradient + caption overlay
- Mono "01" type label

**Photos available**:
- `pastry-case.jpg` — case interior
- `storefront-counter.jpg` — front counter
- `breakfast-burrito.jpg` — burrito hero
- `burrito-chorizo.jpg` — chorizo burrito with salsa
- `burrito-ham-eggs.jpg` — ham & eggs burrito
- `juice-orange.jpg` — fresh OJ
- `pan-dulce.jpg` — pan dulce
- `smoothie-strawberry.jpg` — strawberry smoothie

**Caption template**: `{photo subject}. {one-line note}.`

---

## 6. Holiday / Seasonal (~10% of posts)
**Goal**: Capitalize on calendar moments — Mother's Day, Día de los Muertos, holidays, weekends.

**Format**:
- Themed headline
- Tied to actual menu item

**Example headlines (Mother's Day)**:
- "MOM. PAN DULCE. COFFEE."
- "FOR MOM, FROM THE BAKERY"

**Caption template**: `{occasion} starts at the bakery. open from 5 am · pan dulce trays out.`

---

## Cadence recommendation

- **3–5 posts per week** on the feed
- Mix: 1 menu spotlight + 1 personality + 1 hours/location + 1 hero photo + 1 process post (or rotate)
- Stories more often (3–5/week) — for stories, just use feed posts; no separate generation needed
